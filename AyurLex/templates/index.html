    Ask about Ayurveda patents, rules and herb safety

    Every answer comes from government and legal sources and shows which ones. If the sources do not cover your question, AyurLex says so instead of guessing. You can ask in English, Hindi and other Indian languages.

    General information only. Not legal or medical advice.

    How AyurLex answers

    1. Language check. The writing script tells us if the question is not in English. A glossary turns Hindi and Hinglish terms into English.
    2. Herb detection. Herb names are matched even with typos (for example "ashwaganda").
    3. Intent classifier. A scikit-learn model (TF-IDF with Logistic Regression) decides if the question is about patents, traditional knowledge, licensing, GI and trademarks, medicine information, dosing, or something out of scope.
    4. Hybrid search. BM25 keyword ranking and character n-gram TF-IDF are combined, then boosted by the herb and the topic.
    5. Safety layer. Dose questions get a fixed answer. Emergency, pregnancy, child and interaction notices are added.
    6. Answer. A language model writes from the sources only, or, with no API key, the best passages are quoted as they are.
    7. Guardrails. Any model answer that contains a dose or a wrong citation is replaced by the source text.
