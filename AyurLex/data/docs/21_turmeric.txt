TITLE: Turmeric (Curcuma longa) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: turmeric
TOPIC: medicine_info

Turmeric (Curcuma longa) is known in Sanskrit as Haridra. The rhizome is used in food and in Ayurvedic medicine, traditionally for skin conditions, wounds, inflammation and digestion.

Safety and cautions for Turmeric: Normal cooking amounts are generally considered safe. Concentrated curcumin supplements may increase bleeding risk with blood thinners, may worsen gallbladder problems, and high-absorption products have been linked to rare liver injury. Stop supplements before surgery unless the doctor says otherwise.
