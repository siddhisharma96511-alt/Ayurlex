TITLE: Pharmacovigilance for Ayurvedic drugs and safe purchase
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: law
TOPIC: licensing

The Ministry of AYUSH runs a National Pharmacovigilance Programme for Ayurveda, Siddha and Unani drugs to collect reports of adverse reactions and to monitor drug quality and safety. Patients, doctors and manufacturers can report suspected adverse events.

Ayurvedic preparations that contain processed metals or minerals, such as bhasmas, have raised quality and safety concerns when poorly made. Buy only from licensed manufacturers, check that the label shows a licence number, ingredients and expiry date, and tell your doctor about every Ayurvedic product you take.
