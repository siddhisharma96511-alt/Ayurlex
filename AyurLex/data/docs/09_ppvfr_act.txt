TITLE: Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights Act 2001
SOURCE: https://plantauthority.gov.in
TYPE: law
TOPIC: geo_trademark

The Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights Act, 2001 protects new plant varieties and the rights of farmers and breeders, and is administered by the PPV&FR Authority.

A variety can be registered if it is new, distinct, uniform and stable. A cultivator or breeder who develops a new medicinal plant variety can seek protection. Simply using a wild medicinal plant in a preparation does not create plant variety rights.
