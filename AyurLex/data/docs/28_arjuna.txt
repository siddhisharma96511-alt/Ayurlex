TITLE: Arjuna (Terminalia arjuna) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: arjuna
TOPIC: medicine_info

Arjuna (Terminalia arjuna) is known in Sanskrit as Arjuna. The bark is traditionally used as a heart tonic.

Safety and cautions for Arjuna: People with heart disease, or on heart, blood pressure or blood thinning medicines, must ask their doctor before using it, as it is not a replacement for prescribed heart treatment. Evidence from good clinical trials is limited.
