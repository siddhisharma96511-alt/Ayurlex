TITLE: Tulsi or Holy Basil (Ocimum tenuiflorum) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: tulsi
TOPIC: medicine_info

Tulsi (Ocimum tenuiflorum), also called holy basil, is known in Sanskrit as Tulasi. Leaves are traditionally used for cough, cold and respiratory complaints and as a general tonic.

Safety and cautions for Tulsi or Holy Basil: It may lower blood sugar, so people on diabetes medicines should monitor glucose. It may slow blood clotting, so use caution with blood thinners and before surgery. Large amounts are not advised in pregnancy or when trying to conceive.
