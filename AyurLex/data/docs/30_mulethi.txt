TITLE: Mulethi or Licorice (Glycyrrhiza glabra) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: mulethi
TOPIC: medicine_info

Mulethi (Glycyrrhiza glabra), also called licorice or Yashtimadhu, is traditionally used for cough, sore throat and stomach complaints.

Safety and cautions for Mulethi or Licorice: Regular or high intake of licorice root can raise blood pressure and lower potassium levels, so people with high blood pressure, heart or kidney disease should avoid it unless a doctor agrees. It is not advised in pregnancy. It can interact with diuretics, blood pressure medicines and some heart medicines.
