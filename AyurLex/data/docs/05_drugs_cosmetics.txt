TITLE: Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940 and Rules 1945 - Ayurvedic drugs
SOURCE: https://indiacode.nic.in
TYPE: law
TOPIC: licensing

Ayurvedic, Siddha and Unani (ASU) drugs are regulated under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945. Chapter IVA of the Act deals with ASU drugs including misbranded, adulterated and spurious drugs.

A manufacturer needs a manufacturing licence from the State Licensing Authority. The premises must meet Good Manufacturing Practice requirements for ASU drugs given in Schedule T of the Rules.

Classical Ayurvedic medicines are made according to formulations in the authoritative texts listed in the First Schedule of the Act. Proprietary Ayurvedic medicines are not found in those texts and must have the formula and ingredients declared on the label. Labels must show the manufacturing licence number and the other particulars required by the Rules.

Advertisements of drugs are also controlled by the Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954, which prohibits claims to cure certain listed diseases.
