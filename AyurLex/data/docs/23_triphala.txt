TITLE: Triphala - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: triphala
TOPIC: medicine_info

Triphala is a classical Ayurvedic formulation of three fruits: Amalaki (Phyllanthus emblica), Bibhitaki (Terminalia bellirica) and Haritaki (Terminalia chebula). It is traditionally used for digestion and as a mild laxative. Because it is described in classical texts, Triphala counts as traditional knowledge and cannot be patented as such.

Safety and cautions for Triphala: It can cause loose stools, cramps or diarrhoea. It is not advised in pregnancy unless a practitioner recommends it. People with chronic bowel disease should ask a doctor first.
