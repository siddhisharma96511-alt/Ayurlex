TITLE: Giloy or Guduchi (Tinospora cordifolia) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: giloy
TOPIC: medicine_info

Giloy (Tinospora cordifolia) is known in Sanskrit as Guduchi. The stem is traditionally used as an immune tonic and for fever.

Safety and cautions for Giloy or Guduchi: Cases of drug-induced liver injury have been reported, so avoid long unsupervised use and stop if jaundice appears. It may stimulate the immune system, so avoid it in autoimmune disease or after organ transplant unless a doctor agrees. It may lower blood sugar and should be stopped before surgery.
