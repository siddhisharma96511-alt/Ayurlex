TITLE: Trade Marks Act 1999 - brands and formulation secrets
SOURCE: https://ipindia.gov.in
TYPE: law
TOPIC: geo_trademark

The Trade Marks Act, 1999 protects brand names, logos and packaging that distinguish the goods of one trader from another. Registration gives exclusive use in India for the registered goods and is valid for ten years, renewable.

A mark that is merely descriptive, such as the common name of a herb or a classical formulation, is generally not registrable because it lacks distinctiveness. Choose a coined or distinctive brand.

A trademark protects the brand, not the formula. A formulation can be protected as a trade secret through confidentiality, or by a patent if it meets the patentability requirements.
