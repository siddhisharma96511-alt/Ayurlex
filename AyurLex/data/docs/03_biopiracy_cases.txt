TITLE: Bio-piracy cases: turmeric and neem patents
SOURCE: https://www.tkdl.res.in
TYPE: law
TOPIC: traditional_knowledge

In 1995 the US Patent and Trademark Office granted a patent on the use of turmeric for wound healing. India's CSIR challenged it using written traditional literature as evidence of prior art, and the patent was revoked in 1997.

A European patent on a neem-based fungicide, held by the US Department of Agriculture and the company W R Grace, was opposed by Indian and international groups and was revoked by the European Patent Office. These cases led India to build the TKDL so that examiners can find traditional knowledge as prior art.
