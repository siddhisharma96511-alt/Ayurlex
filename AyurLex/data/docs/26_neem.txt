TITLE: Neem (Azadirachta indica) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: neem
TOPIC: medicine_info

Neem (Azadirachta indica) is known in Sanskrit as Nimba. Leaves and bark are traditionally used for skin conditions, as an antiseptic and for blood sugar balance.

Safety and cautions for Neem: Neem oil taken by mouth can be toxic, especially to infants and children. Avoid in pregnancy and when trying to conceive. Neem may lower blood sugar, so diabetic patients should be careful.
