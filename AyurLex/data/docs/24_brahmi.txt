TITLE: Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: brahmi
TOPIC: medicine_info

Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri) is known in Sanskrit as Brahmi. The whole plant is traditionally used as a medhya rasayana, a tonic for memory and learning. Evidence for benefit is limited.

Safety and cautions for Brahmi: It can cause nausea, stomach cramps and drowsiness. Ask a doctor first if you take sedatives, thyroid medicine or medicines for stomach spasm. Avoid in pregnancy unless a practitioner advises.
