TITLE: Guggul (Commiphora wightii) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: guggul
TOPIC: medicine_info

Guggul (Commiphora wightii), also called Guggulu, is a resin traditionally used in Ayurveda for joint complaints and for cholesterol and weight management.

Safety and cautions for Guggul: It is not advised in pregnancy. It can cause stomach upset, rash and headache in some people. It may interact with thyroid medicine, blood thinners and some heart and blood pressure medicines, so ask a doctor first. Evidence for cholesterol lowering is mixed.
