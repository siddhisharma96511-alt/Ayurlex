TITLE: Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: shatavari
TOPIC: medicine_info

Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus) is known in Sanskrit as Shatavari. The root is traditionally used as a tonic for women's reproductive health and for lactation.

Safety and cautions for Shatavari: It may cause allergy in some people and has a diuretic effect. People with hormone-sensitive conditions or on diuretics should ask a doctor first. Use in pregnancy and breastfeeding only on professional advice.
