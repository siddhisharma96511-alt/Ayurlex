TITLE: Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: ashwagandha
TOPIC: medicine_info

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) is known in Sanskrit as Ashwagandha. The root is the part commonly used. In Ayurveda it is a rasayana (rejuvenative) traditionally used for stress, weakness and vitality. Clinical evidence is limited and still developing.

Safety and cautions for Ashwagandha: Avoid in pregnancy. It may affect thyroid hormone levels, so people with thyroid disease or on thyroid medicine should ask a doctor first. It may add to the effect of sedatives and sleeping medicines. Rare cases of liver injury have been reported, so stop and seek care if you notice jaundice, dark urine or severe tiredness. People with autoimmune conditions should be cautious.
