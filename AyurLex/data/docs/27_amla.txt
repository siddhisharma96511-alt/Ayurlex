TITLE: Amla or Indian Gooseberry (Phyllanthus emblica) - uses and safety
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: medicine
HERB: amla
TOPIC: medicine_info

Amla (Phyllanthus emblica), also called Indian gooseberry, is known in Sanskrit as Amalaki. The fruit is rich in vitamin C and is a main ingredient of Chyawanprash and Triphala. It is traditionally used as a rasayana.

Safety and cautions for Amla or Indian Gooseberry: It may lower blood sugar and may slow clotting, so use caution with diabetes medicines, blood thinners and before surgery. Large amounts may cause acidity in some people.
