TITLE: General safety when using Ayurvedic medicines
SOURCE: https://ayush.gov.in
TYPE: safety
TOPIC: medicine_info

Natural does not always mean safe. Herbs can cause allergies, side effects and interactions with prescription medicines, especially blood thinners, diabetes medicines, thyroid medicines, sedatives and blood pressure medicines.

Tell your doctor about every herbal product you use. Stop herbal supplements before planned surgery unless the doctor advises otherwise. Avoid self-treating serious or long-lasting symptoms. Pregnant and breastfeeding women and children should use herbal products only on professional advice.

Seek urgent medical care for a severe allergic reaction, jaundice, persistent vomiting, chest pain or any serious symptom after taking a herbal product.
